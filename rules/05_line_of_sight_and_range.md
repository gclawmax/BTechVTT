# BattleTech Quick Start --- Line of Sight, Firing Arc and Range

## Line of sight

-   LOS is a straight line from the centre of the attacker's hex to the
    centre of the target's hex.
-   Hexes crossed by that line are intervening hexes.
-   Attacker and target hexes are not intervening terrain.
-   If LOS passes exactly between two hexes, the target's controller
    chooses which hex counts for LOS for all attacks between those
    'Mechs for the remainder of the turn.
-   Intervening 'Mechs do not affect LOS.

## Woods and LOS

-   Light Woods = 1 woods point.
-   Heavy Woods = 2 woods points.
-   3 or more intervening woods points block LOS.

## Firing arc

A target must be inside the attacking 'Mech's weapon firing arc.

## Range

Use the shortest path and count hexes between attacker and target,
including the target hex.

Weapon range brackets are defined by each weapon's record: - Short: +0 -
Medium: +2 - Long: +4 - Target beyond long range: invalid.
