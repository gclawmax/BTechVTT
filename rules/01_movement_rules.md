# BattleTech Quick Start --- Movement Rules

Source: Beginner Box Quick-Start Rules, Movement sections.

## Movement modes

  Mode      MP available Backward?       Attack modifier
  ------- -------------- ------------- -----------------
  Stand                0 No movement                  +0
  Walk        Walking MP Yes                          +1
  Run         Running MP No                           +2
  Jump        Jumping MP Special                      +3

-   Select exactly one mode at the start of that 'Mech's movement.
-   MP spent may not exceed the selected mode's rating.
-   Unspent MP is lost at the end of the phase.
-   A movement mode cannot be changed after it is selected.
-   Standing still keeps the 'Mech in its starting hex and prevents
    movement/facing changes.

## Facing

-   Every 'Mech has one of six facings.
-   Changing facing costs 1 MP per hexside.
-   A 180-degree turn costs 3 MP.

## Ground movement

-   A walking/running 'Mech can move forward into the hex it faces.
-   It can move backward only while walking.
-   To enter another direction, it must first change facing.
-   Backward movement does not change facing.
-   Running cannot move backward.

## Terrain costs

Every entered hex costs at least 1 MP.

  Terrain         Extra MP
  ------------- ----------
  Clear                 +0
  Light Woods           +1
  Heavy Woods           +2

Therefore: - Clear = 1 MP - Light Woods = 2 MP - Heavy Woods = 3 MP

## Stacking

-   A 'Mech may not move through a hex occupied by an enemy 'Mech.
-   A 'Mech may not end movement in a hex occupied by another 'Mech.

## Jumping

-   Jumping uses Jumping MP.
-   1 hex costs 1 Jumping MP.
-   A jump may return to the starting hex for 1 MP.
-   Ignore terrain while jumping.
-   Ignore other 'Mechs while travelling through hexes.
-   Cannot land in an occupied hex.
-   Ignore facing while jumping.
-   The jumper may choose any facing on landing.

## Movement completion

For each 'Mech, retain at least: - movement mode - MP used - hexes
traversed - starting hex - final hex - starting/final facing

These movement results are needed by the later Weapon Attack Phase.
