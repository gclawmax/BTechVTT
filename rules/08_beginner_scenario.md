# BattleTech Quick Start --- Beginner Scenario

## Victory

First side to destroy the opposing 'Mech wins.

If a 'Mech exits the map: - It is considered destroyed. - Unless the
opponent is destroyed in the same turn, the opponent automatically wins.

## Deployment

-   Defender places their Griffin anywhere within 3 hexes of the south
    edge.
-   Attacker places their Wolverine on any hex along the north edge.
-   Both players roll 2D6; winner is the attacker.
-   Defender deploys first.

This scenario is useful as a minimal integration test for the VTT.
