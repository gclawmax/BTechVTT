# BattleTech Quick Start --- Hit Location and Damage

## Hit location

After a successful attack: - Roll 2D6. - Use the target 'Mech's
hit-location table.

Standard hit-location table in these Quick-Start record sheets: - 2:
Center Torso - 3: Right Arm - 4: Right Arm - 5: Right Leg - 6: Right
Torso - 7: Center Torso - 8: Left Torso - 9: Left Leg - 10: Left Arm -
11: Left Arm - 12: Head

## Missile attacks

-   SRM: damage is 2 per missile; roll a separate hit location for each
    missile that hits.
-   LRM: damage is 1 per missile; group hits into 5-point groups, with
    any remainder as one smaller group; roll one hit location per group.
-   Missile cluster size is determined using the Cluster Hits Table.

## Damage resolution

1.  Apply damage to armor in the rolled location.
2.  If armor is exhausted and damage remains, transfer remaining damage
    inward according to the target's damage-transfer diagram.
3.  Continue until all damage is applied.

## Destruction

-   Destroyed right/left torso immediately destroys the corresponding
    arm; the corresponding leg is not damaged.
-   Destroyed leg prevents movement and facing changes for the rest of
    the game, but the 'Mech may still fire.
-   Destroyed head or center torso destroys the 'Mech.
-   Destroyed 'Mechs are removed at the end of the phase in which they
    were destroyed.
