# BattleTech Quick Start --- Sequence of Play

Source: Beginner Box Quick-Start Rules.

## Turn order

1.  Initiative Phase
2.  Movement Phase
3.  Weapon Attack Phase

## Initiative

-   One player per side rolls 2D6.
-   Higher result wins initiative for the turn.
-   Re-roll ties.
-   The side that lost initiative moves first; the winner moves second.
-   In Weapon Attack Phase, the side that lost initiative declares
    attacks first; the winner declares second.

## Movement phase boundary

-   Every controlled 'Mech chooses exactly one movement mode: standing
    still, walking, running, or jumping.
-   A 'Mech may choose not to move.
-   Weapon attacks occur only after all movement is complete.

## Implementation rule

`phase == "movement"` must permit movement actions only. Do not resolve
weapon attacks, hit rolls, damage, or combat during Movement Phase.
