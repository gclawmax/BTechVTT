# MegaMek data attribution

The supported roster is a curated transformation of selected Inner Sphere
3030-era and Clan Invasion-era unit records from the
[MegaMek Data Repository](https://github.com/MegaMek/mm-data).

MegaMek Data © 2025-2026 by The MegaMek Team is licensed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

BT-VTT adapts those unit records into its own browser-game schema and currently
implements only its supported subset of movement, terrain, attacks, heat, and
damage rules. This derived unit data remains non-commercial and is provided
under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.

## Unit artwork

BattleMech token artwork listed in `assets/mechs/manifest.json` is extracted
from an official MegaMek release using its `mekset.txt` and
`imgFileAtlasMap.yml` mappings. Each manifest entry records the original
logical filename, atlas file, and crop coordinates. MegaMek artwork is used
only by this non-commercial fan project under the asset terms shipped with
that release; attribution remains with The MegaMek Team and the individual
artists credited in MegaMek's mapping files.
